## Questions

Research questions for this study:

1. In what contexts (e.g., study goal, habitat sampled, taxa detected) are NAS being reported in the eDNA metabarcoding datasets?
2. What aspects of study design, methods, data interpretation, and data reporting contribute to higher confidence NAS eDNA metabarcoding detections?
3. In which aspects of the eDNA metabarcoding workflow can standards be applied that would be readily generalizable across different types of NAS studies?
4. Which aspects of the eDNA metabarcoding workflow deserve special attention in terms of new guidelines to be done in a certain way, and which aspects are more appropriate as minimum reporting guidelines?
